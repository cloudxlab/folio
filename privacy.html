Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · applies to Folio for iPhone and Android

The short version

  • Folio has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your books, reading progress, highlights, notes and drawings are stored only on your device.
  • Folio never uploads your books. It sends a book’s title and author to look up ratings, and a word you select to look up its meaning, and that is all.
  • We (the makers of Folio) do not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Folio is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • the books you add (EPUB, PDF, text and Markdown files) and books converted from PDF;
  • reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes and drawings;
  • your library details, collections, reading list, reading statistics and goal;
  • your settings, such as theme, font and read-aloud voice.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting Folio from your phone deletes all of it, unless you have made a backup.

When Folio uses the internet

Folio works offline. It connects to the internet only for the features below, and only to these services. It talks to them directly from your phone; nothing passes through a server of ours.

FeatureWhat is sentSent to
Ratings and book details (year, genres) shown in your libraryThe book’s title and authorOpen Library (Internet Archive) and Google Books
Looking up a word or name you selectedThe selected word or phraseFree Dictionary API, Wiktionary and Wikipedia
Opening a link you tap (for example, a book’s Open Library page)The page requestThat website, in your browser

As with any internet request, these services can see your device’s IP address and the request itself. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. Folio sends no identifiers, no account details, and never the contents of your books.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech voices built into your phone (on iPhone, Apple’s voices; on Android, your phone’s speech engine). The text being read is handled by your phone’s operating system. Folio does not send it anywhere. If you choose a voice that your phone marks as an online voice (Android only), your phone’s speech engine may use the internet under its own privacy terms.

Backups

If you create a backup, Folio writes a file to the place you choose, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your phone’s storage. If you turn on automatic backup, Folio updates that same file from time to time. The file contains your reading data and, if you choose, your books. It is stored by whichever service you picked, under that service’s terms; Folio does not upload it anywhere else.

Permissions

  • Files: Folio only sees files you pick or share with it.
  • Notifications (optional): used to show PDF conversion progress or problems, and read-aloud controls on Android. Nothing is sent from your device.
  • Folio does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or tracking permission.

Children

Folio does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Because your data is only on your device, you are in full control of it: you can export highlights and notes, back up or restore your data, delete individual books, or delete the app to remove everything. There is nothing held by us to request, correct or delete.

Changes to this policy

If Folio’s handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on Folio’s GitHub page.
