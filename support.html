Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Report a problem on GitHub.

Adding books

How do I add my own books?

Tap + Add in your library and pick one or more files. You can also send books to Folio from other apps: in Files, Mail or Safari, tap Share and choose Folio. Folio copies the book into its library, so you can delete the original afterwards.

Which formats does Folio read?

EPUB, PDF, plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md). DRM-protected books bought from other stores (for example, Kindle or Apple Books purchases) can’t be opened.

What happens to PDFs?

Folio turns a PDF into a reflowable book on your phone, so the text fits your screen and you can change the font and size. Conversion runs in the background while you keep reading; a long PDF can take a few minutes. On iPhone, keep Folio open until it finishes, because iOS pauses apps soon after you switch away.

Scanned pages that are only pictures (with no text in them) are kept as page images, so you can still read them, but their text can’t be resized, searched or read aloud.

How do I remove a book?

In the library, tap ⋯ on the book and choose to delete it. Your highlights and notes for it are removed too, so export them first if you want to keep them.

Reading

How do I turn pages and open the menu?

Tap near the left or right edge, or swipe, to turn the page. Tap the middle of the page to show the menu with the table of contents, search, settings and more. Pinch to make the text bigger or smaller.

How do I highlight, add a note or look up a word?

Press and hold a word, drag the handles to select more, then choose Highlight, Note or Look up. All your highlights are collected under Notes & highlights, where you can export them to Markdown or Word, or share them.

Read aloud

The voice sounds robotic. How do I get a better one?

iPhones come with compact voices only. Better, natural-sounding voices are a free download from Apple:

  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices (on some iOS versions: Spoken Content → Voices).
  2. Choose your language and download a voice marked Premium or Enhanced, for example Ava (Premium) or Zoe (Premium).
  3. Close Folio completely and open it again. It picks the best voice automatically, or choose one under Read aloud → Voice.

On Android, install voices from your phone’s text-to-speech settings (often Google’s speech engine), then pick one in Folio.

Does read aloud keep going when the screen is locked?

Yes. You can pause, play and skip from the lock screen or your headphones. Use the sleep timer to stop after a set time or at the end of the chapter.

Your data

How do I move my library to a new phone?

Open the menu, choose Backup & restore and choose Back up library and save the file to iCloud Drive or another place you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install Folio, open Backup & restore and choose to restore that file. Your books, progress, highlights and notes come back.

Is my reading data backed up automatically?

No. Everything stays only on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it. Use Backup & restore → Back up library from time to time to save your books, progress, highlights and notes to a file you keep, and always before deleting the app or changing phones.

Does Folio collect any of my data?

No. There’s no account, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy for the few things Folio looks up online, such as ratings and word definitions.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Helpful details: your phone model, iOS or Android version, the Folio version (shown on its App Store or Google Play page), and what you did just before the problem. Please don’t attach books you don’t have the right to share.
