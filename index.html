Everything a reader needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Folio does the reading well and stays out of the way.

Reads what you have

EPUB, PDF, plain text and Markdown. PDFs are turned into reflowable books on your phone, so text fits the screen instead of making you pinch and pan.

Highlights, notes, ink

Highlight in colour, add notes, even draw on the page with a pen or marker that stays put when the text reflows. Export everything to Markdown or Word.

Read aloud

Listen with your phone’s voices while the page follows along. A sleep timer and lock-screen controls make it perfect for bedtime.

Look it up

Select a word for its definition, or a name for its Wikipedia summary, without leaving the page. Search the whole book in an instant.

Stats & goals

See how much you read, keep a gentle daily goal, and line up what’s next with a reading list in the order you want.

Backups you own

Save your progress, highlights and books to a file wherever you like (iCloud Drive, for example), or turn on automatic backup.

A page turn that feels like paper

Swipe and the page curls under your finger, following it all the way. Prefer something quieter? Choose a slide, or turn the animation off, or scroll instead.

  • Tap the edges or swipe to turn, tap the middle for the menu
  • Two pages side by side in landscape
  • Pinch to change the text size

Typography you’ll want to read

Proper hyphenation and justification, generous margins, and fonts made for long reading: Literata, EB Garamond, Source Serif and more.

  • Paper, Sepia, Dusk and Night themes, or follow the system
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic for easier reading
  • Adjustable size, spacing and margins

Close your eyes and keep reading

Read aloud uses the voices on your phone, so it works offline. Folio highlights each sentence as it’s spoken and turns the pages for you.

  • Speed control and a sleep timer, including “end of chapter”
  • Keeps going with the screen locked
  • Play and pause from the lock screen or headphones

Your books stay on your phone.

No account to create and nothing to sign in to. Folio has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your library, progress, highlights and notes are stored on your device and nowhere else.
